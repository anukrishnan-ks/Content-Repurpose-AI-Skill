# Content Repurposer

Turn one piece of long-form content into ready-to-post versions for each platform.

## Step 1: Understand the source
Before writing anything, identify:
- **Core message**: the one idea a reader should remember (one sentence)
- **3 strongest points**: facts, stories, or tips worth keeping
- **Best hook**: the most surprising, useful, or emotional line in the piece
- **Audience**: who this is for

If the user hasn't said which platforms they want, produce all three below.

## Step 2: Write each version

### LinkedIn
- Length: 150–300 words (hard limit 3,000 characters)
- Open with a hook line that works on its own, because only the first 2 lines show before "see more"
- Short paragraphs of 1–2 sentences, with blank lines between them
- Professional but personal. Share a lesson or insight, not just a summary
- End with a question to invite comments
- 3–5 relevant hashtags at the very end

### Instagram (caption + carousel)
- **Caption**: 100–150 words (hard limit 2,200 characters), friendly and conversational, emojis used sparingly
- First line must grab attention, because it gets cut off quickly
- End with a call to action ("Save this for later", "Share with a friend who…")
- 8–15 hashtags, mixing broad and niche, on a separate line
- **Carousel**: 5–7 slides, each with a headline of at most 8 words plus one short line
  - Slide 1 is the hook; the last slide is the call to action

### X (Twitter)
- **Single post**: under 280 characters, the sharpest version of the core message
- **Thread**: 4–7 posts, each under 280 characters
  - Post 1 is the hook and should make people want to read on
  - One idea per post
  - The final post summarizes and links back to the original
- 0–2 hashtags at most

## Step 3: Quality check
Before delivering, confirm that:
- Each version reads like it was written *for* that platform, not copy-pasted
- No facts were invented. Use only what is in the source
- Every character limit is respected
- The core message is the same across all versions

## Output format
Use a clear header for each platform. Put each post in its own block so it's easy to copy. After the posts, add one line suggesting the best platform to post on first and why.
